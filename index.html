UES Website Work Log

A plain-language record of the work done on universalenvironmentalservices.com.

Prepared by Manny Pachano for Juan Fritschy and Lisa Sandefur.

Last updated: August 4, 2026

What this page is. It lists every change made to the website, in order, with what each one means for the business. It is written for people who do not work with websites.

Traffic and ranking figures are not on this page. Those are covered in the monthly reports.

1What we found when we started

These are the issues found during the first review. Most are normal for a site that ran for years without a technical check. None were caused by recent activity.

What we foundWhy it matters
The site answered to four different web addressesSearch engines had to guess which one was the real site. That splits the site's credit instead of pooling it.
A full second copy of the site was publicGoogle was reading a duplicate at a different address. Time Google spends on a copy is time it does not spend on the real pages.
The site's automatic task scheduler had stoppedBackups, updates and other scheduled jobs were stuck and not running.
Visitors' browsers were told to remember nothingEvery repeat visitor downloaded the whole site again. That makes the site slower than it should be.
The Locations page links to none of the state pagesThe state pages are hard for visitors and for Google to find. They earn traffic despite this.
Some pages have near duplicate copiesTwo similar pages compete with each other instead of one strong page ranking well.
Main page headings are set at the wrong levelGoogle reads headings to understand what a page is about. Right now most pages do not clearly say it.
Empty list pages are being shown to GoogleAuthor and category pages with nothing useful on them are taking up Google's attention.
The software the site runs on is out of dateIt no longer receives security fixes. This is the site's most serious open item.
The site's design theme is from 2020The theme has to be updated before the software above can be updated safely.
Arizona and New Jersey listings show the Georgia phone numberLocal customers see an out of state number. It also weakens those listings in local search.
The Old Bridge, New Jersey listing is suspendedThat location does not appear on Google Maps at all right now.
The footer still read 2017 to 2022Small, but it makes the site look unattended to anyone who notices.

2Work completed

Every item below is finished and checked. Where a change was made to the live site, it was tested before and after.

DateWhat we didWhat it means for you
Late July Agreed the focus areas with Juan Two target regions confirmed. The Washington DC to Newark corridor at county level, and Phoenix within 70 miles. All work points at these.
Late July Reviewed all 29 Google Business listings 27 are live and verified. Two are waiting on video verification. We now know the exact state of every listing.
Late July Measured local map rankings in Arizona and New Jersey Casa Grande ranks 3rd of 89 nearby competitors. Old Bridge does not appear at all, because of the suspension. This is our before picture.
August 3 Verified every audit finding against live Google data Each item on this page was confirmed directly with Google Search Console and Analytics. Nothing here is based on assumption.
August 3 Told the website its own correct address The site was working out its address from however each visitor arrived. It now knows one fixed address. This fixed the root cause of the four address problem.
August 3 Completed a full review of the site's admin area Every plugin, setting, page and menu is now documented. No changes were made during this review. It was read only.
August 3 Updated the footer copyright year The footer read 2017 to 2022. It now reads 2026.
August 4 Reduced the four web addresses to one All versions of the address now send visitors and Google straight to the same place, in one step. Cleaner for search engines and slightly faster for visitors.
August 4 Closed the public duplicate copy of the site The second copy now redirects to the real site. Google can stop reading it and spend that time on the pages that matter.
August 4 Restarted the site's automatic task scheduler Scheduled jobs run again, every five minutes. Backups and scheduled work are no longer stuck.
August 4 Turned on browser memory for images and design files Repeat visitors no longer download the whole site again. Pages load faster on the second visit onward.
