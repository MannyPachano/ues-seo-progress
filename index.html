UES Website Work Log

A plain-language record of the work done on universalenvironmentalservices.com.

Prepared by Manny Pachano for Juan Fritschy and Lisa Sandefur.

Last updated: August 4, 2026

What this page is. Each row below is a problem we found and what we did about it. It is written for people who do not work with websites.

Traffic and ranking figures are not on this page. Those are covered in the monthly reports.

1Problems solved

Most of these are normal for a site that ran for years without a technical check. None were caused by recent activity. Where a change was made to the live site, it was tested before and after.

The problemWhat we did about itWhen
The footer still read 2017 to 2022 Updated it to 2026. Small, but an out of date footer makes the whole site look unattended. Auto-updates every year. August 3
The site answered to four different web addresses, so search engines had to guess which was the real one Fixed in two steps. First the site was told its own fixed address, which was the underlying cause. Then all four addresses were pointed to that one address, in a single hop. The site's credit now pools instead of splitting. August 3 and 4
A full second copy of the site was public at a different address Closed it. That address now sends visitors and Google to the real site. Time Google spent reading a duplicate goes back to the pages that matter. August 4
The site's automatic task scheduler had stopped, so backups and updates were stuck Restarted it. Scheduled jobs now run every five minutes. August 4
Visitors' browsers were told to remember nothing, so every repeat visit downloaded the whole site again Turned on browser memory for images and design files. Pages load faster from the second visit onward. August 4
